Caribbean Literacy Village

Every building in this village holds a literacy idea. Some teach reading as a set of skills. Others show reading and writing as part of everyday Caribbean life. Click a building to read or listen to the big idea, try the classroom strategy, answer a short check-in to earn a passport stamp, and write your own reflection.
